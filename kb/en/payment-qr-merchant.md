# QR payments for your shop

QR payments let a buyer pay by scanning a code with their bank app instead of typing card details. It is a good fit for in-person sales, where you want the customer to pay quickly from their own phone.

## Enable QR payments

1. Open **Settings** in the Panel.
2. Go to **Payment methods**.
3. Enable QR payments.
4. Save your changes.

Once enabled, a **one-time QR code** appears on the payment page for every transaction. The buyer opens their bank app, scans the code, and confirms the payment there. Each QR code is tied to a single payment, so it cannot be reused.

## Using QR for in-person sales

For face-to-face sales, generate a payment link from the Panel and show its QR to the customer:

1. Create a payment link in the Panel for the amount you want to charge.
2. Open the link's payment page.
3. Show the QR code on your screen, or share the link so the buyer opens it on their phone.
4. The buyer scans the QR in their bank app and pays.

Payment links are valid for 72 hours, so you can prepare them ahead of time. Once paid, the transaction appears under **Payments** in the Panel and flows into your normal payout cycle.

If a QR code does not scan, the buyer can open the same payment link directly and pay without scanning. For help enabling the method, contact us via a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
