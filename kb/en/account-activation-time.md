# How long does activation take

After you submit your registration, kremzaPay verifies your account before you can accept live payments. Verification (KYC) normally takes **1–2 business days** once we have received a complete set of documents.

The most common reason for delay is incomplete or unclear documentation. To avoid waiting longer than necessary:

1. Make sure your company data matches your registry entry (KRS or CEIDG).
2. Upload legible copies of every requested document.
3. Confirm that the person submitting the application is authorised to represent the company.

If something is missing, our team will contact you through the Panel and the process pauses until you respond. Once all documents are complete and correct, verification usually finishes within the 1–2 business day window.

You do not have to wait idle. A **sandbox environment is available immediately** after registration, so you can integrate and test your setup while verification is in progress. Sandbox transactions are simulated and use test API keys, so nothing is charged and no real money moves. When your account is approved, you simply switch to your live keys.

If your verification has been open for more than two business days and you have not heard from us, check the Panel for pending requests, then open a support ticket. Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET, via the Panel or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo.
