# kremzaPay Panel overview

The kremzaPay Panel is where you manage everything related to your account. It is organised into five main sections, each grouping a set of related tasks.

**Payments** shows every transaction that passes through your account, together with its current status. Use this section to search for a specific payment, review its details, and monitor whether it was authorised, completed, or declined.

**Refunds** is where you return funds to your customers. You can issue a full or partial refund against an original payment within the 180-day refund window and track the status of each refund you create.

**Payouts** covers the transfers of your settled funds to your bank account. Payouts follow a T+1 schedule with a minimum of 10 PLN, and this section lets you review upcoming and completed transfers.

**Reports** brings together your financial records: settlement summaries, invoices, and data exports. Use it to reconcile your accounts, download settlement files, and pull the figures your bookkeeping needs.

**Settings** holds your account configuration, including:

- **API keys** for connecting your integration.
- **Notifications** for transaction and system alerts.
- **Payment methods** available to your customers.
- **Company data** such as name and address.
- **Security**, including two-factor authentication (an authenticator app using TOTP).

If you cannot find something, start from the section that matches the task, or open a support ticket from the Panel. Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
