# Reporting an incident properly

A well-prepared incident report lets us find the cause faster and reduces the back-and-forth. When you open a ticket from the Panel, include the information below.

**What to include**

- **What happened.** Describe the problem in plain terms — for example, payments failing, webhooks not arriving, or the Panel not loading.
- **When it happened.** Give the times, and always state the **timezone**. A precise time window lets us match your report to our logs.
- **Affected identifiers.** List the relevant **sessionIds** or **payout ids**. These let us locate the exact transactions instead of searching blind.
- **What you already checked.** Tell us the steps you have taken — for example, that you checked the status page and worked through the webhook checklist. This prevents us from suggesting things you have already tried.
- **Screenshots.** Attach screenshots of any error messages or unexpected states.

**One ticket per incident**

Open a single ticket for each incident. Putting everything in one place keeps the full history together and lets us track the investigation cleanly.

**Updates arrive on the ticket**

Once your ticket is open, replies and progress updates are posted directly to it. There is no need to create a second ticket to chase an answer — duplicate tickets for the same incident slow things down by splitting the context. Check your existing ticket for the latest status, and add any new details there.
