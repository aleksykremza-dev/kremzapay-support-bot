# Payout history

The **Payouts** section of the Panel keeps a full record of every payout kremzaPay has sent for your shop. Use it to reconcile your bank account, track pending transfers, and investigate anything that looks off.

## What each entry shows

Every payout in the list includes:

- **Status** — scheduled, sent, or returned.
- **Amount** — the total paid out, in the shop currency.
- **Bank account** — the destination account for that payout.
- **Linked report** — the report that details the payments making up the payout.

Open any entry to see the report behind it, so you can trace exactly which payments were settled.

## Payout statuses

- **Scheduled** — the payout is queued and will be processed on its cycle date.
- **Sent** — the funds have been released to your bank.
- **Returned** — the transfer came back, usually because the bank account was wrong or could not accept it.

## Returned payouts

When a payout is returned, the money is **credited back to your balance** — it is not lost. To recover it:

1. Open the returned payout and check the bank account details.
2. Correct the account in your payout settings.
3. Leave the balance as is.

The returned amount is then included in your **next cycle** and paid out again automatically with the corrected account.

If a payout stays returned after you fix the account, contact us via a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
