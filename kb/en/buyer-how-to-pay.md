# How to pay with kremzaPay

When you check out at a shop that uses kremzaPay, you are taken to a secure payment page to complete your order. Paying takes just a few steps.

1. At checkout, choose kremzaPay as the payment option. You are redirected to the kremzaPay payment page.
2. Pick a payment method:
   - **BLIK** — enter the 6-digit code from your banking app. The code is valid for 2 minutes.
   - **Card** — enter your card details. Your bank may ask you to confirm with 3D Secure 2.
   - **Fast bank transfer (pay-by-link)** — select your bank and approve the payment in your online banking.
   - **Google Pay** or **Apple Pay** — confirm with your device.
3. Confirm the payment in your bank or wallet if prompted.
4. After the payment is approved, you are redirected back to the shop, which shows your order confirmation.

Good to know:

- The payment page stays open for 60 minutes. If you do not finish within that time, the session expires and you will need to start the payment again from the shop.
- A BLIK code is valid for 2 minutes; if it runs out, generate a new one in your banking app.
- You can pay in PLN, EUR, USD, GBP, or CZK, depending on what the shop offers.

If a payment does not go through, see the article on failed payments. For anything else, the shop where you ordered is your first point of contact.
