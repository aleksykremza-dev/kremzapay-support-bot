# Changing company data

You can update your company details in the Panel under **Settings → Company**. This is where kremzaPay keeps the information used on your invoices, settlements, and account records, so it should always match your official registry entry.

To request a change:

1. Go to **Settings → Company**.
2. Open the field you need to update, such as name or address.
3. Enter the new details.
4. Attach a current registry document that confirms the change (KRS for companies or CEIDG for sole traders).
5. Submit the request.

Changes to your company name or address require a current registry document because we have to verify the update against an official source. Make sure the document is recent and legible, and that it clearly shows the new details. Requests are usually **processed within 1–2 business days**. Until the change is approved, your existing data stays in place.

Some changes cannot be made this way. A change of **NIP (tax identification number)** effectively means a different legal entity, which requires a new agreement rather than a simple edit. If your NIP is changing, do not submit it through the form — contact support instead so we can prepare the new agreement.

If a request is rejected, we will explain why through the Panel, usually because the document was missing, outdated, or did not match the details you entered. Correct the issue and submit again.

For help, open a support ticket from the Panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
