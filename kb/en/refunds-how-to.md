# How to refund a payment

You can refund a completed payment for up to 180 days after it was completed. The money goes back to the same card or account, in the same currency as the original payment.

To issue a full refund:

1. Sign in to the kremzaPay Panel.
2. Open the **Payments** section and find the transaction you want to refund. You can search by amount, date, or order reference.
3. Open the payment. Its status must be **completed** — you cannot refund payments that are still pending, authorized, failed, or expired.
4. Click **Refund**.
5. Leave the amount at the full payment value for a complete refund.
6. Confirm the refund.

The refund is created immediately and appears in the **Refunds** section, where you can track its status. The customer receives the money within 1–5 business days, depending on their bank or card issuer.

A few things to keep in mind:

- Refunds are always returned to the original payment method. You cannot redirect a refund to a different card or account.
- Any fees related to a refund follow the terms in your agreement.
- If you only need to return part of the amount, use a partial refund instead (see the partial refunds article).

If a payment is older than 180 days or you cannot locate it, contact us through a ticket in the Panel or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
