# Payouts in foreign currencies

kremzaPay pays you out in the currency of your shop. Supported currencies are PLN, EUR, USD, GBP and CZK, and each payout is sent in the same currency your shop earns.

For this to work smoothly, connect a bank account held in that currency. When your shop currency matches your bank account currency, the funds arrive without any conversion.

## When account currency differs

If your bank account is held in a different currency than your shop, kremzaPay still sends the payout in the shop currency. Your bank then converts the incoming amount to the account currency **at its own exchange rate**. kremzaPay does not perform this conversion and does not control the rate your bank applies, so the final amount depends entirely on your bank.

To avoid conversion costs and rate uncertainty, use a bank account that matches your shop currency.

## One payout per currency per day

Each currency is settled separately. You receive at most **one payout per currency per day**. If your shop takes payments in more than one currency, each currency is grouped and paid out on its own, so you may see several payouts on the same day — one for each currency.

You can review every payout, its currency and its destination account in the **Payouts** section of the Panel. Each entry links to the report behind it.

The minimum payout amount is 10 PLN or the equivalent threshold in the relevant currency. If you need help matching accounts to currencies, contact us via a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
