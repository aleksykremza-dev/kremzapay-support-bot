# Enabling payment methods

Each shop in kremzaPay has its own set of active payment methods. You decide which options your buyers see on the payment page, and you can adjust them at any time.

## Available methods

- BLIK
- Cards (Visa, Mastercard)
- Fast bank transfers (pay-by-link)
- Google Pay
- Apple Pay

## How to enable a method

1. Go to **Settings → Payment methods**.
2. Select the shop you want to configure.
3. Use the toggle next to each method to turn it on or off.
4. Save your changes.

The updated set of methods applies to new payments right away. Payments already in progress are not affected.

## Verification before going live

Some methods may require additional verification before they can accept live payments. When this applies, the method stays visible in your settings but is marked as pending until the check is complete. You can continue configuring other methods in the meantime.

If a method remains pending longer than expected, open a ticket from the Panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.

## Good to know

- Methods are configured per shop, so different shops can offer different options.
- Turning a method off does not affect payments that were already completed with it.
- Buyers only see methods that are active and fully verified for the shop.
