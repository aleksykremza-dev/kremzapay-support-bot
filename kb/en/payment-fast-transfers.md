# Fast bank transfers (pay-by-link)

Fast bank transfers, also known as pay-by-link, let buyers pay directly from their online banking without typing account numbers or transfer titles by hand.

## How it works for the buyer

1. On the payment page, the buyer chooses **Fast transfer** and selects their bank.
2. They are redirected to their bank's login screen.
3. The transfer form is already pre-filled with the amount, recipient, and title.
4. The buyer confirms the transfer in their banking app or website.

Because the details are filled in automatically, there is no risk of a wrong amount or a missing payment title.

## Timing and confirmation

Confirmation usually arrives within minutes. Once the bank confirms the transfer, the payment moves to **completed**.

If the bank confirmation is delayed, the payment stays in the **pending** status until the confirmation reaches kremzaPay. You do not need to do anything; the status updates automatically when the bank responds. Remember that a payment session lasts 60 minutes, after which an unconfirmed payment becomes **expired**.

## Supported banks

Fast transfers are available only for Polish banks. Buyers with accounts outside Poland should use another method, such as a card, BLIK, Google Pay, or Apple Pay.

If a transfer stays pending far longer than expected, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
