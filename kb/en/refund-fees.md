# Fees and refunds

When you refund a payment, the original processing commission is not returned to you. Under your agreement, the commission is charged for handling the original transaction, and that work has already been done regardless of whether the payment is later refunded.

The refund itself carries no extra charge. Issuing a refund — full or partial — does not add a new fee on top of the original commission. You can refund a payment without worrying about a separate cost for the refund operation.

Refunded amounts are deducted from your next payout. kremzaPay does not ask you to send money back for each refund; instead, the total of your refunds is subtracted from the balance due on your next scheduled payout. Payouts run on a T+1 basis, with a minimum payout of 10 PLN.

A short worked example:

1. A buyer pays for an order, and the commission is charged as usual.
2. You later refund that order in full from **Refunds**.
3. The refunded amount is subtracted from your next payout balance.
4. The original commission stays as it was — it is not credited back.

Because refunds reduce your upcoming payout, it is worth keeping an eye on your balance in **Reports** if you process many refunds in a short period, so a payout is not delayed by an insufficient balance.

For questions about how a specific refund affected your payout, open a ticket in the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET).
