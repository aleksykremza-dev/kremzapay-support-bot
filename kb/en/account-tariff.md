# Where to see your pricing plan

Your pricing at kremzaPay is individual and set out in your agreement. You can review it at any time in the Panel under **Settings → Agreement**.

To check your plan:

1. Go to **Settings → Agreement**.
2. Open your current agreement.
3. Review the terms that apply to your account.

This is the authoritative place to confirm what you have agreed to. The commission charged on your transactions is applied according to your agreement, so if you want to know exactly what applies to your account, this section is where to look rather than relying on general information.

Because terms are individual, the figures shown here reflect what was agreed for your business specifically. If you operate under a negotiated arrangement, that is what appears in **Settings → Agreement**.

If your pricing ever changes, you will not have to discover it by chance. Changes are **announced in advance by email and in the Panel**, so you always have notice before anything takes effect. Keep your notification settings and contact email up to date so these announcements reach the right person.

If anything in your agreement is unclear, or you believe the terms shown do not match what you expected, open a support ticket from the Panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo. Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET. Please note that pricing questions are answered against the agreement on file, so having your account details ready will help us respond quickly.
