# Where is my refund

A refund always starts with the shop. kremzaPay cannot begin a refund on its own, so the merchant has to initiate it from their side first. Once the shop initiates the refund, it is sent back to the same card or account you used for the original payment.

After the merchant initiates the refund, it usually reaches you within 1–5 business days. The exact timing depends on your bank or card issuer. Refunds are returned in the same currency as the original payment, and a merchant can initiate a refund within 180 days of the payment.

If you are waiting for a refund, follow these steps:

1. **Ask the shop first.** Confirm that the merchant has actually initiated the refund and on which date. This is the most common reason a refund seems missing.
2. **Count business days.** Allow up to 5 business days from the initiation date, not from the day you requested it.
3. **Check the right account.** The money returns to the original card or account, which may not be the one you check most often.
4. **Contact support.** If more than 5 business days have passed since the shop confirmed initiation, open a ticket in the panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo. Include the payment id so we can locate the transaction.

Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET. Having the payment id ready makes it much faster to check the status of your refund.
