# Payouts on weekends and holidays

kremzaPay uses a T+1 payout cycle, and T+1 always counts **business days only**. Weekends and Polish public holidays are skipped when the bank processes your money, which changes the day your funds actually land.

Here is how it works in practice:

1. Payments captured on a business day are queued for the next business day.
2. If that next day is a weekend or a public holiday, the bank processing moves to the following business day.
3. Payments captured on Friday therefore arrive on Monday, or on Tuesday when Monday is a holiday.

Polish public holidays shift the cycle in the same way. During long weekends or holiday clusters, several payouts can settle on the first business day afterwards.

One important detail: your payout **schedule in the Panel is not changed** by weekends or holidays. The Panel still shows your regular cycle, and your balance keeps accumulating as normal. What moves is only the bank processing date, because banks do not settle transfers on non-business days.

To check when a specific payout is expected, open **Payouts** in the Panel and look at the status and date for each entry. Scheduled payouts show the planned processing day; once the bank sends the funds, the status changes to sent.

If a payout looks delayed beyond the next business day, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
