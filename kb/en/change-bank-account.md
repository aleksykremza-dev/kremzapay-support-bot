# Changing your payout bank account

You can update the bank account that receives your payouts at any time. Because this change affects where your money is sent, it requires extra security confirmation.

## How to change the account

1. Sign in to the kremzaPay Panel.
2. Go to Settings → Payouts.
3. Enter the new bank account details.
4. Confirm the change with two-factor authentication.
5. Attach a confirmation document, such as a bank statement header or a transfer confirmation showing the account holder and number.

## What happens next

- The request is processed within 1–2 business days.
- Payouts are paused until the new account is verified. Payments continue to be collected as usual; only the transfer of funds is held.
- Once the new account is verified, payouts resume automatically on the normal schedule.

## Security note

kremzaPay never asks you to change your bank account by email. Account changes can only be made by you in the Panel under Settings → Payouts, protected by two-factor authentication. If you receive an email or message asking you to update payout details, do not act on it and report it to us.

If you need help, open a ticket from the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri 8:00–18:00 CET).
