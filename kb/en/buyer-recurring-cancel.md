# Cancelling recurring payments

A recurring payment is an agreement between you and the shop (the merchant). The merchant stores a card token and charges it on a schedule — for example every month. kremzaPay processes each charge, but the shop decides when the charges happen and for how much.

Because the agreement is with the shop, cancel it there first:

1. Log in to your account on the shop's website.
2. Open your subscription, plan or billing settings.
3. Select the option to cancel or stop the recurring payment.
4. Keep the confirmation the shop sends you.

Once the shop cancels the agreement, no further charges will be sent to kremzaPay.

If you cannot reach the shop, or the shop does not respond, your bank can also help. Most banks let you block or remove the card token used for recurring charges:

1. Open your bank app or contact your bank.
2. Find the recurring or subscription payments section.
3. Block the payment to that shop, or ask the bank to stop the card token.

Blocking the card token stops future charges from going through, even if the shop still tries to charge you. Note that blocking only prevents new charges — it does not automatically refund charges that already completed, and it does not end your contract with the shop. You may still owe the shop under the terms you agreed to.

If a charge was taken after you cancelled and you believe it was an error, contact the shop for a refund. Refunds are possible within 180 days of the original payment.
