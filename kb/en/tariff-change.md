# When your pricing changes

From time to time kremzaPay may update the terms that apply to your account. When that happens, you are always told in advance — pricing never changes without notice.

How the process works:

1. **Advance notice.** Any change is announced at least 14 days before it takes effect. You receive the notice by email and see a banner in your Panel, so there are two independent ways to catch it.
2. **The notice states the effective date.** You will know exactly when the new terms begin to apply.
3. **Continued use means acceptance.** If you keep using kremzaPay after the effective date, the updated terms apply to your account.
4. **If you disagree, act before the date.** Should the new terms not suit you, contact support before the effective date so your case can be handled while the current terms still apply.

At any moment you can review the terms that are in force. Open **Settings → Agreement** — it always shows your current conditions, both before and after a change takes effect. Comparing the banner announcement against Settings → Agreement is the quickest way to see exactly what is changing.

Because notices arrive by email and in the Panel, it is worth making sure the email address on your account is current and monitored, so you never miss a 14-day window.

To raise a concern about an upcoming change, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET. Reaching out before the effective date gives the most room to resolve things.
