# Paying by QR code

Paying by QR code lets you move a payment from one screen to your phone. This is useful when you start the purchase on a computer but want to confirm it in your banking app. The QR code shown on the kremzaPay payment page is one-time and tied to your specific payment.

How to pay:

1. On the kremzaPay payment page, choose the **QR code** option.
2. Open your bank's mobile app and go to its QR scanning feature.
3. Scan the QR code shown on the payment page.
4. Your bank app fills in the payment details. Review them.
5. Confirm the payment in the app.

Once you confirm, the payment page updates and the status changes to completed. kremzaPay then sends an email confirmation with the payment id.

Things to know:

- The QR code is valid for the same 60-minute payment session. If the session runs out before you confirm, the payment ends as expired and the code no longer works.
- The code is one-time. Do not save it or reuse it for another purchase.
- Only scan the QR code from the genuine kremzaPay payment page. Check the page address before scanning, and never scan a payment QR code sent to you by someone else.

If the code will not scan, make sure your app is up to date and that the whole code is visible on screen. You can also return to the payment page and choose another method while the session is active.
