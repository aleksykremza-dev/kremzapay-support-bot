# Complaining about a shop

kremzaPay processes payments for shops, but the shops are separate businesses responsible for their own products, prices, delivery and customer service. If you have a problem with an order, the shop is the right place to start.

**Step 1 — Contact the shop.**

1. Use the contact details on the shop's website — its help page, contact form or email.
2. Describe the problem clearly: what you ordered, what went wrong and what you expect (delivery, repair, refund).
3. Give the shop reasonable time to reply.
4. Keep copies of your messages.

Most problems — late delivery, wrong item, no reply yet — are solved directly with the shop.

**Step 2 — Contact kremzaPay.**

If the shop does not resolve the matter, and you believe the shop is acting unfairly or breaking the rules, you can report it to us. Email **pomoc@kremzapay.demo** and include:

- the **payment id** from your kremzaPay confirmation email;
- the shop's name;
- a short description of the problem and what you have already tried.

The payment id lets us find the transaction quickly, so please include it. We review merchants that violate our terms and can take action against shops that break them. This may include contacting the shop or, in serious cases, ending its ability to accept payments through kremzaPay.

Please note that kremzaPay cannot force a shop to deliver goods, and reporting a shop is not the same as a refund or a card chargeback. If you paid by card and the goods never arrived, you can also raise the matter with your bank.

We answer messages Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET, by email or through a Panel ticket.
