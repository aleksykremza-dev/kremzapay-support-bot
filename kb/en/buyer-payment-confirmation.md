# Getting a payment confirmation

After your payment reaches the completed status, kremzaPay sends you an email confirmation. This message confirms that the payment was received and processed successfully.

The confirmation email includes:

- The amount and currency you paid.
- The name of the shop you paid.
- The **payment id**, a unique reference for the transaction.
- The date and time of the payment.

Keep the payment id, because support uses it to find your transaction quickly if you ever need help with a refund or a status question.

It is important to know that the kremzaPay email confirms the **payment**, not the order. The shop sends its own order confirmation separately, with details such as delivery, invoice, or product information. If you receive the payment confirmation but not the order confirmation, contact the shop directly, since order handling is on their side.

If you did not receive a payment confirmation:

1. Check whether the payment actually reached the completed status. Payments that end as failed or expired do not generate a confirmation.
2. Look in your spam or junk folder.
3. Confirm the email address you used at checkout was correct.
4. If the payment completed but no email arrived, open a ticket in the panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo.

Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET. Have the shop name and approximate payment time ready if you cannot find the payment id.
