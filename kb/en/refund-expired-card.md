# Refund to an expired or closed card

You can issue a refund even if the buyer's card has expired or been replaced. The refund is sent to the card number used for the original payment, and the bank reroutes it to the account behind that card. You do not need the buyer's new card details.

To issue the refund:

1. Open **Refunds** in the Panel.
2. Find the original payment and select **Refund**.
3. Enter the amount (full or partial) and confirm.

The refund status moves from **created** to **processing**. Because the card is expired, the bank routes the money to the underlying account rather than the card itself, so settlement can take longer than usual — up to 14 days in these cases.

If the account behind the card has also been closed, the bank has nowhere to deliver the funds and rejects the transfer. When this happens, the refund status changes to **rejected**. The amount is not delivered to the buyer, and no further automatic retry is made.

If a refund shows as **rejected**, contact us so we can confirm the outcome and advise on next steps. You may need to arrange the return with the buyer directly, for example by a standard bank transfer to an account they still hold.

Refunds are always made in the original currency of the payment. Partial refunds remain possible and can be repeated up to the original amount.
