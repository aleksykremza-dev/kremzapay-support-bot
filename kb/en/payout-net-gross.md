# Net vs gross amounts

Every payout has two figures: a **gross** amount and a **net** amount. Understanding the difference makes your bookkeeping straightforward.

**Gross** is the sum of all completed payments included in the payout. It represents the full value your customers paid before anything is deducted.

**Net** is what actually reaches your bank account. It is the gross amount minus all applicable deductions.

The deductions applied between gross and net are:

1. **Commission** — charged per your agreement with kremzaPay.
2. **Refunds** — any refunds issued are subtracted from the payout.
3. **Chargeback deductions** — amounts related to chargebacks are removed.

So the relationship is simply:

**net = gross − commission − refunds − chargeback deductions**

Both figures are shown for each payout in the **Reports** section. To review them:

1. Open **Reports** in your panel.
2. Select the payout you want to inspect.
3. Review the gross total, the itemised deductions, and the resulting net amount.

Because refunds and chargebacks are deducted at payout time, a single payout can occasionally show deductions that relate to payments from earlier days. This is expected — the report always ties each deduction to the transaction it came from, so you can trace every difference between gross and net.

If the net amount that arrived in your bank does not match the net shown in Reports, contact support through a panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET).
