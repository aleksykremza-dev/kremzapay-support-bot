# Merchant registration and verification (KYC)

To accept payments with kremzaPay, complete the registration form and pass identity verification (KYC). Verification confirms who you are and where funds are settled, which protects both you and your customers.

## What you need

Prepare the following before you start:

- Company data, including NIP and REGON for Polish companies.
- An identity document for the person representing the company.
- Confirmation of the bank account that will receive your payouts.

## Registration steps

1. Open the online registration form and enter your business details.
2. Provide the company data (NIP and REGON for Polish companies).
3. Upload the representative's identity document.
4. Confirm the bank account used for payouts.
5. Submit the form for review.

## Verification time

Verification typically takes 1–2 business days. You will be notified in the kremzaPay Panel when your account is approved or if we need additional information.

## Working while you wait

You do not have to sit idle during review. While verification is pending, you can run test payments in the sandbox environment to configure and check your integration. Live payments become available once your account is verified.

If you have a question about a submitted document, open a ticket from the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri 8:00–18:00 CET).
