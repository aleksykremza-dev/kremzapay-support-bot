# Is paying with kremzaPay safe

kremzaPay is built to keep your payment details protected from the moment you open the payment page until the payment is complete. This page explains the main safeguards and how you can stay safe as a buyer.

What kremzaPay does:

- **Encrypted connection.** All data on the payment page travels over an encrypted connection, so your details cannot be read in transit.
- **3D Secure 2 for cards.** Card payments use 3DS2, which lets your bank confirm that the transaction is really you, usually with a one-time code or in-app approval.
- **Bank-side login.** For BLIK and fast bank transfers, you approve the payment inside your own banking app or on your bank's page. kremzaPay never sees your banking password.

What kremzaPay will never do:

- kremzaPay will never email you asking for your full card number, CVV code, BLIK code, or banking password.
- kremzaPay will never ask you to move money to a different account to "release" or "verify" a payment.

How you can stay safe:

1. **Check the address.** Before paying, confirm the payment page address is the genuine kremzaPay page and that the connection is secure.
2. **Keep codes private.** A BLIK code and any one-time codes are for you alone. Never share them, and remember a BLIK code is valid for only 2 minutes.
3. **Be careful with links.** Only pay through the page opened from the shop, not from unexpected messages.

If something looks wrong, do not confirm the payment. Open a ticket in the panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET).
