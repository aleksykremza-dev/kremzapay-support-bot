# Your agreement and terms

Your use of kremzaPay is governed by the merchant agreement you accepted online when you registered. There is no paper contract to sign: during registration you review the terms and confirm acceptance before your account is activated.

## Where to find the current version

You can read the agreement that applies to your account at any time:

1. Sign in to your Panel.
2. Open **Settings → Agreement**.
3. The current version is displayed there, along with the date it took effect.

Keep a copy for your records if you need one; the page can be printed or saved from your browser.

## How changes are announced

From time to time we update the terms, for example to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. When this happens:

- We announce the change **14 days in advance**.
- The notice is sent to your account email address.
- A banner also appears in your Panel so you do not miss it.

The 14-day period gives you time to review what is changing before it applies. Once the new version takes effect, it replaces the previous one and is shown in **Settings → Agreement**.

## If you have questions

If anything in the agreement is unclear, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET. We are happy to explain a clause, but we cannot provide legal advice about how the terms apply to your specific business.
