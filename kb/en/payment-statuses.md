# Payment statuses explained

Every payment in the kremzaPay Panel moves through a defined set of statuses. You can see the current status of any transaction in the **Payments** section. This article explains what each one means.

## pending

The payment has been created, but the bank has not confirmed it yet. This is normal right after a customer starts paying. Wait a few minutes and refresh the transaction — the status usually updates on its own once the bank responds.

## authorized

The funds have been blocked on the customer's account, but not yet captured. The payment is reserved and waiting for capture. Once capture completes, the status changes to completed.

## completed

The payment is confirmed and the money is settled. Completed payments are included in your next payout according to your payout schedule. You can issue a refund against a completed payment from the **Refunds** section.

## failed

The bank rejected the payment. The customer was **not** charged. This can happen for many reasons, such as insufficient funds or a declined verification. The customer can simply try again with a new payment.

## expired

The payment session timed out before it was paid. An expired payment cannot be paid — the customer needs to start a new payment. No funds are taken.

## Need help

If a status looks wrong or a transaction is stuck, open a ticket from the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri 8:00–18:00 CET).
