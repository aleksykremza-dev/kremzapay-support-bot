# Exports for your accountant

kremzaPay gives you ready-to-use exports so your accountant can reconcile your payments without manual retyping. Everything is available under **Reports → Export** in the Panel.

You can export three documents:

1. **Monthly invoice CSV** — the same positions as your PDF invoice, in a machine-readable format.
2. **Settlement reports** — the detailed record of what was settled during the period.
3. **Payout list** — every payout made to your bank account, with its identifier.

The columns are designed for reconciliation, not just display. Across the exports you will find the fields an accountant needs to match kremzaPay's figures against your bank statements and books:

- **Dates** — when the payment and payout occurred.
- **Gross** — the amount before deductions.
- **Commission** — the commission charged, per your agreement.
- **Net** — the amount after the commission.
- **Payout id** — the identifier that ties each settled amount to the actual transfer to your bank.

Because the payout id appears both in the export and against the transfer, your accountant can trace any line from the books straight to the money that arrived.

A simple monthly routine works well:

1. Open **Reports → Export** after the invoice is issued (by the 7th).
2. Download the monthly invoice CSV, the settlement report, and the payout list for the previous month.
3. Hand the package to your accountant as one set.

Doing this every month keeps your records aligned and makes period-end closing straightforward.
