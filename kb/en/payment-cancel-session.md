# Cancelling an unpaid payment

If a customer started a payment but has not paid, you can cancel the payment session yourself instead of waiting for it to expire. This is useful when an order is abandoned, duplicated, or created by mistake.

## How to cancel an unpaid payment

1. Sign in to your Panel.
2. Open **Payments**.
3. Find and open the payment you want to cancel. It must still be unpaid (for example, in the pending status).
4. Select **Cancel**.

Once cancelled, the session moves to a closed state and can no longer be paid.

## If you do nothing

You do not have to cancel manually. An unpaid session **expires automatically after 60 minutes**. When it expires, its status changes to expired and it behaves the same as a cancelled session.

## Cancelled and expired sessions cannot be paid

A payment session that has been cancelled or has expired is final — the customer cannot return to it and complete payment. If the customer still wants to pay, **create a new payment session** and share the new link.

This is why it is safe to cancel abandoned or duplicate sessions: doing so does not block a genuine customer, who can simply be given a fresh payment.

If a payment appears stuck or you are unsure of its status, check its current status in **Payments**, or contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
