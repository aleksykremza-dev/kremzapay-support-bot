# Representment — answering a dispute

Representment is your formal response to a chargeback. When a customer disputes a payment, you have the right to answer by submitting evidence that the transaction was valid. This is your main chance to recover the funds.

How representment works:

1. **Open the dispute.** Go to the **Payments** section of the Panel and open the disputed payment. The dispute view shows the reason given and the deadline.
2. **Submit your evidence within 14 days.** Attach the documents that support the transaction — order confirmation, delivery tracking with signature, correspondence with the buyer, terms acceptance, and matching buyer and delivery data. Then confirm submission.
3. **kremzaPay forwards it to the issuer.** We package your response and send it to the customer's card issuer, which reviews it against the cardholder's claim.
4. **Wait for the decision.** The issuing bank typically decides within 30 to 60 days. You do not need to do anything during this period.
5. **Check the outcome.** The result appears in the dispute view for that payment.

If you **win**, the disputed amount stays with you. If you **lose**, the amount plus a dispute fee — charged per your agreement — is deducted from your next payout.

Two points decide most cases. First, meet the **14-day deadline**; a late or missing response means the dispute is settled against you automatically. Second, submit **complete, legible evidence** the first time, because you cannot supplement it later. A strong, well-organised package is the difference between recovering the payment and absorbing the loss.
