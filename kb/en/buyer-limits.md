# Payment limits

If a payment is declined for exceeding a limit, the limit almost always comes from your own bank, not from kremzaPay. Banks set separate limits for different payment methods:

- **BLIK limits** — your bank sets a daily amount and sometimes a per-transaction cap for BLIK.
- **Card limits** — your bank sets limits for online and contactless card payments, which may differ from ATM limits.

These limits protect your account and are controlled entirely by your bank.

On the kremzaPay side, the default accepted range for a single transaction is **1 PLN to 50 000 PLN**. A payment below or above this range cannot be processed on the payment page. Individual shops may set their own, narrower limits within this range, depending on what they sell.

If your payment is refused because of a bank limit, you can usually raise the limit yourself:

1. Open your bank app or online banking.
2. Go to the card, BLIK or security settings.
3. Find the section for transaction or daily limits.
4. Increase the limit and save the change.
5. Return to the payment page and try again within the 60-minute session.

Some banks apply the new limit immediately; others need a few minutes. If the change does not take effect, contact your bank directly, as only they can adjust these limits.

If your payment is within your bank's limits and within the 1–50 000 PLN range but still fails, the problem may be elsewhere — for example an expired card or a declined authorization. In that case contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
