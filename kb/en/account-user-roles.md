# Team roles and access

kremzaPay lets you give colleagues access to the Panel with a role that matches their responsibilities. Every person works under their own login, so you always know who did what.

There are three roles:

- **Admin** — full access to everything, including API keys and payouts. Admins can manage the team, change company data, and configure security. Give this role only to people who genuinely need complete control.
- **Finance** — access to Reports, Refunds, and invoices. This role suits accountants and finance staff who reconcile settlements and handle returns, but should not manage keys or payouts.
- **Viewer** — read-only access. Viewers can see payments and reports but cannot make changes, issue refunds, or move funds.

To invite someone:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Enter the person's email address.
3. Select the role you want to assign.
4. Send the invitation.

The invited person receives an email and sets up their own login. Each account has its own credentials and its own two-factor authentication (an authenticator app using TOTP), which every user must enable. Access is never shared, so you should not pass one login between several people.

You can change someone's role or remove their access at any time from **Settings → Team**. Review your team periodically and remove anyone who no longer needs access. If you need help, open a support ticket from the Panel, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
