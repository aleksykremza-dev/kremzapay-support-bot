# Holds and rolling reserves

A **rolling reserve** is a portion of your balance that kremzaPay temporarily holds back rather than paying out immediately. It is a standard risk-management measure and does not mean anything is wrong with your account.

Reserves are applied in situations of **elevated risk**, for example:

- An increased **chargeback rate** on your account.
- A **new merchant** that has started processing high volume quickly, before a settled payment history exists.

When a reserve applies, part of each payout is set aside and held **per your agreement** with kremzaPay. The exact percentage and holding period are defined in that agreement.

The held amount is always **visible separately** in your panel:

1. Open the **Payouts** section.
2. The reserve is shown as its own line, distinct from your available and pending balances.
3. This lets you see at a glance how much is held and how much is on its way to your bank.

Reserves are **released automatically** once the agreed holding period has passed. You do not need to request the release, and when the period elapses the funds become part of your normal available balance and are paid out on the usual T+1 business day cycle.

As your account builds a stable history and your risk indicators improve, reserve terms may be reviewed under your agreement.

If the reserve line does not match your expectations, or you are unsure of the holding period that applies to you, raise a ticket in the panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET).
