# Closing your account

You can close your kremzaPay account whenever you no longer need it. Closure is requested from the Panel, so you stay in control of the timing and can prepare beforehand.

Before you begin, download anything you want to keep. Go to **Reports** and export your settlement summaries, invoices, and transaction data. Once the account is closed you will no longer have access to the Panel, so keep these files for your records.

To start the process, open a support ticket from the Panel and request account closure. Here is what happens next:

- **Payments stop immediately.** As soon as closure is requested, your account can no longer accept new payments, and your live API keys stop processing transactions.
- **Refunds remain possible for 30 days.** So you can still handle customer returns, refunds can be issued for 30 days after closure is requested.
- **The final payout is made after settlement.** Any remaining balance is paid out once outstanding transactions have settled, within 30 days.

Make sure your bank account details are correct before you request closure, so the final payout reaches you without delay. If you have pending disputes or unsettled transactions, they may need to clear before the final payout can be completed.

If you are closing because of a problem rather than because you no longer need the service, consider opening a support ticket first — we may be able to help. Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET, via the Panel or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo.
