# My payment failed — what now

If your payment failed, it means your bank or card issuer rejected it. A failed payment is not charged to you, so it is safe to try again.

Common reasons a payment is rejected:

- The BLIK code was entered incorrectly or expired after its 2-minute validity.
- The card details were wrong, or the card was declined by the bank.
- 3D Secure 2 confirmation was not completed.
- The payment session was open longer than 60 minutes and expired.

What to do:

1. Return to the shop and start the payment again.
2. Choose a payment method and, if needed, generate a fresh BLIK code or re-enter your card details.
3. Complete any confirmation your bank asks for.

## If money left your account but the order is unpaid

Sometimes your bank shows an amount as taken even though the shop reports the order as unpaid. This is usually a temporary authorization hold, not a completed charge. The funds return to your account automatically within 1–3 business days. You do not need to do anything, and you should not be charged twice for the same order.

If the money has not returned after 3 business days, contact the shop where you placed the order, and share the date, amount, and payment method. The shop can look up the payment in the kremzaPay Panel and follow up with kremzaPay support if needed.
