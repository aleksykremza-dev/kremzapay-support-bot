# Refund after the 180-day window

kremzaPay allows refunds within 180 days of the original payment. After this window closes, a refund through kremzaPay is technically impossible. The payment can no longer be selected for refund in **Refunds**, and the Panel will not accept a refund request against it.

This limit comes from how card and bank networks link a refund back to the original transaction. Once too much time has passed, that link is no longer available, so the funds cannot be routed automatically through kremzaPay.

If you still need to return money to a customer after 180 days, settle it directly with them outside the platform. The usual approach is:

1. Agree the amount to return with the customer.
2. Collect their current bank account details.
3. Send the amount by your own standard bank transfer.
4. Keep the transfer confirmation for your records.

Because this return happens outside kremzaPay, it will not appear in **Refunds** and will not be deducted from your payout. Retaining the confirmation is important, as it is your only proof that the money was returned should the customer or an auditor ask later.

Before the 180-day window closes, refunds work normally: they are made to the same card and currency, statuses move from **created** through **processing** to **completed** or **rejected**, and partial refunds can be repeated up to the original amount.

If you are unsure whether a payment is still within the window, open a ticket in the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET).
