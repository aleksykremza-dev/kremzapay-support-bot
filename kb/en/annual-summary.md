# Annual summary

The annual summary is a single document that pulls together a whole year of activity, so you don't have to gather twelve separate invoices when closing your books. It is prepared each January for the year just ended and is available from **Reports → Annual summary** in your Panel.

The summary presents totals per month, so you can see how the year developed and check each month at a glance. For every month it includes:

1. **Gross** — the total value of payments processed.
2. **Commissions** — the commission charged, per your agreement.
3. **Refunds** — the value refunded to customers.
4. **Chargebacks** — chargeback activity for the month.
5. **Payouts** — the total paid out to your bank account.

Because the figures are the yearly rollup of what already appears on your monthly invoices and in **Reports**, the annual summary reconciles with the documents you have collected through the year. It is meant as an overview and a cross-check, not a replacement for the monthly invoices themselves.

This makes it useful for two things in particular: yearly closing, where you need consolidated totals in one place, and tax filing, where a per-month breakdown of gross, commissions, refunds, chargebacks and payouts supports your accountant's work.

You can download the annual summary as both PDF (for records and sharing) and CSV (for import into accounting software).

To get it:

1. Open **Reports** in the Panel.
2. Select **Annual summary**.
3. Choose the year and download PDF or CSV.

If a total doesn't match your own records, compare it against the relevant monthly invoices first. For anything still unclear, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
