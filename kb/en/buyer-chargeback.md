# Disputing a card payment (chargeback)

A chargeback is a way to reverse a card payment through your bank when something goes wrong with a purchase — for example the goods never arrived, or the shop charged you for something you did not receive and does not respond to your messages. It is handled by your bank and the card network, not by kremzaPay directly.

Always try to solve the problem with the shop first. A chargeback should be a last resort, used when the shop cannot or will not help.

**Step 1 — Contact the shop.**

1. Write to the shop describing the problem clearly.
2. Ask for the goods to be delivered or for a refund.
3. Keep copies of your messages and any reply (or the lack of one).

Many issues are solved here. If the shop agrees to a refund, kremzaPay can return the payment within 180 days of the original transaction.

**Step 2 — Contact your bank.**

If the goods never arrived and the shop does not respond within a reasonable time, ask your bank to open a chargeback:

1. Call your bank or use your banking app.
2. Explain that you paid but did not receive the goods.
3. Provide the date, amount, shop name and any proof you have.
4. Follow the bank's instructions and wait for their decision.

From there, your bank runs the whole chargeback process, contacts the card network, and decides the outcome. Timelines and rules are set by the bank and the card network.

It helps to have your payment details ready — the amount, date and the payment id from your kremzaPay confirmation email. If you need those details, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
