# Payout schedule

kremzaPay transfers your collected funds on a regular schedule. This article explains when payouts are sent and what can shift the date.

## Default timing

Completed payments are paid out on a T+1 business day basis. This means funds from a payment that completes today are scheduled for payout on the next business day.

## Minimum payout amount

The minimum payout is 10 PLN. If your available balance is below 10 PLN on a scheduled payout day, the amount rolls over and is added to the next payout. It continues to accumulate until the balance reaches the minimum.

## Weekends and holidays

Payouts are only sent on business days. If a payout would fall on a weekend or a public holiday, it shifts to the next business day. Because of this, a payment completed just before a long weekend may be paid out slightly later than usual.

## Where to check

You can see your upcoming and past payouts in the kremzaPay Panel under Payouts. Each entry shows the scheduled date, the amount, and the current status, so you always know what has been sent and what is still pending.

If a payout looks delayed beyond the expected schedule, check the status in the Panel first, then contact support if needed.
