# VAT on kremzaPay services

kremzaPay's payment service fees are subject to VAT in accordance with Polish law. The commissions and other charges you pay for using the service are treated as a service, and VAT is applied to them just as it would be for any other service you buy for your business.

Your monthly invoice shows the VAT clearly. For each period you will see three figures:

1. **Net** — the value of the services before VAT.
2. **VAT** — the tax charged on that net value.
3. **Gross** — the total you are actually charged, which is net plus VAT.

This layout means you do not have to calculate anything yourself — the invoice already separates the taxable base from the tax.

kremzaPay's VAT number is shown on every invoice, so your accountant always has the issuer's tax identification on hand.

For your bookkeeping, there is nothing unusual to do. Your accountant books the kremzaPay invoice the same way they book any other service invoice you receive: the net value goes to costs, and the VAT is handled according to your company's VAT position. If your business is entitled to deduct input VAT, the VAT on kremzaPay's invoice is treated like any other deductible service invoice.

The invoice is available by the 7th of the following month, as both PDF and CSV, from **Reports** in your Panel.

If you have a specific question about how the VAT is shown, the best first step is to hand the invoice to your accountant. For issues with the invoice itself, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
