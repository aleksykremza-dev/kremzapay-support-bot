# In what currency will I pay

Every payment through kremzaPay is charged in the currency the shop has configured. A shop can be set up in one of five currencies:

- PLN (Polish złoty)
- EUR (euro)
- USD (US dollar)
- GBP (British pound)
- CZK (Czech koruna)

The amount and currency you are about to pay are always shown on the payment page before you confirm. Check them there so you know exactly what will be charged.

**If your card is in the same currency as the shop**, the amount is charged directly, with no conversion.

**If your card is in a different currency**, your bank converts the amount into your card's currency. The conversion rate and any related bank fees are set by your bank, not by kremzaPay. This means the final amount on your statement may differ slightly from the figure shown on the payment page, because the shop's price is fixed in its currency and your bank applies its own rate at the moment of settlement.

A few things to keep in mind:

1. The shop always decides the currency of the sale — you cannot change it on the payment page.
2. If your card or the payment page offers to convert the amount for you, compare the rate with your bank's before accepting.
3. For the exact rate applied to your payment, check your bank statement or ask your bank.

kremzaPay does not add its own currency conversion on top of the shop's price. If the amount charged looks wrong, first compare the payment page figure with your bank's conversion. For other questions, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
