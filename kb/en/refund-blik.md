# Refunding a BLIK payment

A payment made with BLIK is refunded the same way as any card payment in kremzaPay. The refund goes to the bank account linked to the buyer's BLIK, so there is no need to collect any account number or additional details from the buyer.

To refund a BLIK payment:

1. Open **Refunds** in the Panel.
2. Locate the original BLIK payment and select **Refund**.
3. Enter the amount (full or partial) and confirm.

The refund status moves from **created** to **processing**, and then to **completed** once the bank settles it. As with other refunds, this usually takes 1–5 business days. The exact timing depends on the buyer's bank.

The buyer does not need to do anything. There is no code to enter and no confirmation to approve in their banking app — the funds simply arrive in the account behind their BLIK. If the buyer asks, you can reassure them that no action is required on their side.

Refunds are made in the original currency of the payment. Partial refunds are supported and can be repeated up to the original amount, which is useful when you refund an order line by line.

If a BLIK refund does not arrive within 5 business days, check the status in **Refunds** first. If it still shows **processing** or **rejected**, open a ticket in the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET).
