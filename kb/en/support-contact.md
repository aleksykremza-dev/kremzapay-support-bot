# Contacting kremzaPay support

If you need help, kremzaPay support is available through two channels. Choose the one that suits you and include the right details so we can respond quickly.

## Ways to reach us

- **Ticket from the Panel (fastest).** Open a ticket directly in the kremzaPay Panel. Each ticket is assigned a ticket number so you can track its progress and see replies in one place. This is the recommended channel.
- **Email.** Write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo if you prefer email.

There is no phone support.

## Support hours

Support is available **Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET**. Requests sent outside these hours are handled on the next business day.

## What to include in your request

To help us resolve your issue on the first reply, include:

- Your **merchant_id**.
- The relevant **sessionId** or **payout id**, if your question is about a specific payment or payout.
- A clear description of the problem and, where helpful, what you expected to happen.

The more precise these details are, the faster we can locate the transaction and respond.

## Response time

You can typically expect a first reply **within 1 business day**. If your question relates to an ongoing dispute or a failed payment, providing the relevant identifiers up front helps avoid back-and-forth and speeds up resolution.
