# Payment methods available to buyers

When you pay at a shop that uses kremzaPay, you choose how to pay on the kremzaPay payment page. The page opens after you confirm your order and stays active for 60 minutes.

The following methods are available:

1. **BLIK** — Enter the 6-digit code from your banking app. The code is valid for 2 minutes, and you confirm the payment in the app.
2. **Card (3DS)** — Pay with a Visa or Mastercard. Card payments are protected by 3D Secure, so your bank may ask you to confirm the transaction with a one-time code or in-app approval.
3. **Fast bank transfer (pay-by-link)** — Pick your bank on the payment page, log in, and confirm a transfer that is already filled in for you.
4. **Google Pay** — Pay with a card saved in your Google account on supported devices.
5. **Apple Pay** — Pay with a card saved in your Apple Wallet on supported devices.

Not every shop enables every method, so you will only see the options the shop has turned on. Supported currencies are PLN, EUR, USD, GBP, and CZK.

After a successful payment the status changes to completed and kremzaPay sends you an email confirmation with the payment id. The shop confirms your order separately.

If a payment does not go through, you can return to the payment page and try another method while the session is still active. If you need help, open a ticket in the panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET).
