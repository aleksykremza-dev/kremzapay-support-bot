# Refund statuses

Every refund you create moves through a series of statuses so you can see exactly where the money is. All refunds and their current status are visible in the **Refunds** section of the Panel.

## The statuses

- **created** — The refund has been registered in kremzaPay but not yet sent to the bank. This is the starting point for every refund, whether full or partial.
- **processing** — The refund has been sent to the bank and is being handled. At this stage the money is on its way back to the buyer.
- **completed** — The money has been returned to the buyer's card or account. The refund is finished and no further action is needed.
- **rejected** — The refund could not be completed. A common reason is that the buyer's account or card is closed, so the bank cannot return the funds.

## Timing

A refund normally reaches **completed** within 1–5 business days, depending on the buyer's bank. Refunds can be created within the 180-day window from the original payment.

## If a refund is rejected

A **rejected** status means the funds did not reach the buyer. Do not assume the buyer received the money. Contact support through the Panel ticket system or pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri 8:00–18:00 CET) so the case can be reviewed and an alternative return arranged where possible.

Check the **Refunds** section regularly while a refund is in **created** or **processing** so you notice a rejection promptly.
