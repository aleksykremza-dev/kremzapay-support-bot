# Fraudulent purchases and disputes

Fraud is one of the most common reasons a payment turns into a dispute. When a card is used without the owner's permission, the real cardholder eventually notices the charge and asks their bank to reverse it — which usually arrives as a chargeback.

**Stolen-card purchases almost always become chargebacks.** The genuine owner did not authorise the payment, so the issuer sides with them. You may never see the goods again, and the amount plus a dispute fee can be deducted from your next payout. Prevention matters far more than defence here.

**3DS2 shifts liability to the issuer.** When a payment is authenticated through 3D Secure 2, responsibility for fraud on that transaction moves to the issuing bank. If an authenticated payment is later claimed to be fraudulent, the bank generally bears the loss rather than you. Keeping 3DS2 active on your checkout is one of the strongest protections available.

Practical steps to reduce fraud losses:

1. **Ship only to verified data for high-value orders.** Match the delivery address and contact details against the payment data, and hold or review orders where they do not line up.
2. **Watch for warning signs** — rushed high-value orders, mismatched billing and delivery countries, or many attempts on one card.
3. **Report confirmed fraud patterns to support.** If you notice a repeating scheme — the same address, device, or product being targeted — tell us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo so it can be examined.

You cannot eliminate fraud entirely, but authenticated payments, careful shipping rules, and early reporting keep the losses small and the disputes manageable.
