# The dispute process step by step

When a customer raises a chargeback, the payment goes through a structured dispute process. Here is what happens at each stage and what is expected from you.

## 1. Notification in the Panel

The dispute begins when kremzaPay notifies you in the Panel. The affected payment is flagged with the disputed amount and the reason given by the customer's bank. Review this information first so you understand what is being challenged.

## 2. Submit your evidence (14 days)

You have **14 days** from the notification to submit evidence that the payment was legitimate. Strong evidence includes:

- An order confirmation showing what was purchased.
- Proof of delivery or that the service was provided.
- Correspondence with the customer relating to the order.

Gather documents that directly address the bank's stated reason. If you do not respond within the 14-day window, the dispute is likely to be resolved in the customer's favor by default.

## 3. Bank decision (typically 30–60 days)

After you submit your evidence, the customer's bank reviews the case and makes the final decision. This review usually takes **30–60 days**. kremzaPay does not decide the outcome; the card issuer does.

## Outcomes

- **Dispute won:** the funds stay with you. The temporary hold is resolved in your favor and no further action is needed.
- **Dispute lost:** the disputed amount is deducted from your next payout.

You can track the status of the dispute in the Panel throughout the process. To reduce disputes, keep clear records of orders, deliveries, and customer communication.
