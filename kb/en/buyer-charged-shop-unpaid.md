# Money left my account but the shop shows unpaid

Sometimes money is blocked on your account, but the shop still shows the order as unpaid. This usually means the payment did not finish successfully, even though your bank placed a temporary hold on the funds.

A payment can end in one of two unsuccessful statuses:

- **failed** — the payment was rejected before it could complete.
- **expired** — the 60-minute payment session ran out before the payment was confirmed.

When a payment ends as failed or expired after your bank has blocked the funds, the block is released automatically. In most cases the money returns to your account within 1–3 business days. No action is needed from you, and you do not need to contact the shop to release the hold.

What you can do:

1. Check your bank app to confirm whether the amount is a real charge or only a pending block.
2. Wait 1–3 business days for a pending block to disappear on its own.
3. If more than 3 business days pass and the money has not returned, contact your bank, because the hold is managed on their side.

If you want to complete the purchase, place the order again and pay within the 60-minute session. If you need help, open a ticket in the panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET). Include the payment id if you have it.
