# Refund timing and the 180-day window

This article explains how long a refund takes to reach the customer and how long a payment stays eligible for a refund through kremzaPay.

## How long a refund takes

Once you confirm a refund in the kremzaPay Panel, it is created immediately. The customer receives the money within 1–5 business days. The exact time depends on their bank or card issuer, not on kremzaPay.

Refunds always return to the same card or account used for the original payment, and in the same currency. You cannot send a refund to a different payment method or convert it to another currency.

## The 180-day window

A payment can be refunded through kremzaPay for 180 days after it was completed. Within that window you can issue full or partial refunds as needed.

After 180 days a refund through kremzaPay is no longer possible. The **Refund** option is no longer available for that payment. If you still need to return money to the customer after this period, you must settle it directly with them outside kremzaPay — for example by bank transfer.

To avoid this, review returns and disputes well before the 180-day limit and process refunds while the payment is still eligible.

If you are unsure whether a payment is still within the window, open it in the **Payments** section, or contact us through a ticket in the Panel or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
