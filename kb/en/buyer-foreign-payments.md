# Paying from abroad

You can pay a Polish shop through kremzaPay from outside Poland. Which methods are available depends on where your card or bank account is held.

**Foreign cards.** Cards issued outside Poland are accepted, as long as they support 3DS2 (also called 3-D Secure 2). This is the standard step where your bank confirms the payment — usually through your banking app, an SMS code or a fingerprint. If your card does not support 3DS2, the payment cannot be completed and you should use a different card.

**Fast bank transfers.** Fast (instant) transfer options on the payment page work only with Polish banks. If you do not hold an account at a Polish bank, choose card payment instead.

**Language.** The kremzaPay payment page is available in Polish and English. You can switch language using the selector on the page, so you can read every step even if you do not speak Polish.

A few practical points when paying from abroad:

1. Make sure online and international payments are enabled on your card in your bank app.
2. Complete the 3DS2 confirmation before the 60-minute payment session expires.
3. If your card is in a different currency, your bank converts the amount at its own rate — the shop still charges in its configured currency.

If a foreign card is declined even though it supports 3DS2, the decision usually comes from your card issuer. Contact your bank first. For other issues with a kremzaPay payment, reach us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
