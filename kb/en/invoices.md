# Finding your invoices

kremzaPay issues you a monthly invoice for the commission on your processed payments. You can find and download all of your invoices from the Panel.

## Where to find them

1. Log in to the kremzaPay Panel.
2. Open the **Reports** section.
3. Select **Invoices**.

Here you will see a list of your invoices, ordered by month. Each entry shows the billing period and the amount.

## When invoices are issued

Your invoice for a given month covers the commission on payments processed during that month. It is issued by the **7th of the following month**. For example, the invoice for January is available by 7 February.

## Download formats

Each invoice is available in two formats:

- **PDF** — a formatted document suitable for your records and accounting.
- **CSV** — a data file you can import into a spreadsheet or accounting software.

Choose the format you need next to each invoice in the list.

## About the commission amount

The commission on your invoice is calculated according to your agreement with kremzaPay. The invoice reflects the payments processed in the billing period and the commission that applies to them under that agreement. If you have questions about the figures on a specific invoice, contact support and include the invoice period and your `merchant_id`.
