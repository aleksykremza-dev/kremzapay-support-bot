# Paying by fast bank transfer (pay-by-link)

A fast bank transfer, also called pay-by-link, lets you pay directly from your bank account without copying account numbers or transfer details by hand. kremzaPay prepares the transfer for you, so you only need to log in and confirm.

How to pay:

1. On the kremzaPay payment page, choose **Fast bank transfer**.
2. Pick your bank from the list shown.
3. You are redirected to your bank's login page. Log in as you normally would.
4. Review the transfer. The recipient, amount, and title are already filled in.
5. Confirm the transfer in your bank, using the method your bank requires (for example a one-time code or in-app approval).

Do not change the pre-filled details. Editing the **amount** or the **transfer title** can stop kremzaPay from matching your payment to the order, which may leave it showing as unpaid even though the money left your account.

After you confirm, you are returned to the shop and the payment status updates to completed. kremzaPay then sends an email confirmation with the payment id.

Keep in mind:

- The payment session stays active for 60 minutes. Finish the transfer within that time, or it may end as expired.
- Not every bank is available for every shop, so you will only see the banks the shop supports.
- If your payment does not complete, you can return to the payment page and try again or choose another method while the session is active.

If you need help, open a ticket in the panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET).
