# How your commission is calculated

kremzaPay charges a commission on each successfully processed payment. The commission is set by your individual agreement — there is no single public rate, and the exact terms depend on what you and kremzaPay signed.

Because the terms are individual, this article does not state any figures. To see the commission that applies to you, open **Settings → Agreement**, where the current conditions are always available.

A few points worth understanding:

1. **The commission is applied per payment.** Every payment that goes through carries its own commission, calculated according to your agreement.
2. **It may differ by payment method.** Different payment methods can have different commission terms, so two payments of the same amount may be charged differently depending on how the customer paid.
3. **It is visible per payment.** Open **Reports** to see the commission deducted from each individual payout. Nothing is hidden — every deduction is itemised.
4. **It is summed on the invoice.** All commissions for a calendar month are added together and shown on your monthly invoice, issued by the 7th of the following month.

Keep in mind that a refund does not return the original commission — the commission stays charged even when the payment is later refunded.

If you want to review or confirm your exact terms, always start in **Settings → Agreement**. If anything there is unclear, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
