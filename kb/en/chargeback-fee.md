# Chargeback fees

When a customer disputes a payment through their bank or card issuer, that dispute is called a chargeback. kremzaPay applies a handling fee for each chargeback, set according to your individual agreement. This article does not state any amount — your exact fee is defined in **Settings → Agreement**.

A few important points:

1. **The fee is charged per dispute.** Each chargeback carries its own handling fee, regardless of the payment's value.
2. **It applies whether you win or lose.** The fee covers the handling of the dispute itself, so it is charged even when the dispute is resolved in your favour.
3. **It is deducted from your next payout.** You do not pay it separately — it is taken from the next scheduled payout after the chargeback is processed.
4. **It appears on your monthly invoice.** Every chargeback fee for the period is listed as its own position on the invoice, issued by the 7th of the following month.

You can see chargeback fees both per payout in **Reports** and totalled on the invoice, so the figures always reconcile.

The most effective way to lower these costs is to have fewer disputes in the first place. Common measures include clear billing descriptors, prompt customer service, and quickly issuing refunds where a dispute would otherwise follow. For practical steps, see the **chargeback-reduce** article.

If a chargeback fee looks incorrect, first check the matching entry in Reports. If it still doesn't add up, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
