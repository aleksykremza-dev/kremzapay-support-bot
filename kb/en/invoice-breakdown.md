# Understanding your monthly invoice

kremzaPay issues one invoice per month, covering the previous calendar month. The invoice is available by the 7th of the following month and can be downloaded from your Panel as both PDF and CSV.

The invoice groups your charges into clear positions:

1. **Commissions per method.** Total commission charged, broken down by payment method, so you can see how each method contributed.
2. **Chargeback fees.** Handling fees for any disputes raised during the month, charged according to your agreement.
3. **Corrections.** Adjustments that apply to the period, such as reversals or manual corrections.

Every figure on the invoice can be traced back to your data. The totals match the sum of the per-payout deductions you see in **Reports** — the invoice is simply the monthly rollup of what was already itemised there. If you want to verify a position, open Reports for the same month and add up the relevant deductions; the numbers should reconcile.

To find your invoice:

1. Open **Reports** in the Panel.
2. Select the month you want.
3. Download the invoice as PDF (for your records) or CSV (for your accounting software).

The PDF is the human-readable document; the CSV contains the same positions in a format your accountant can import.

If a position looks unexpected, compare it against the matching entries in Reports before contacting us. If it still doesn't add up, reach out through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
