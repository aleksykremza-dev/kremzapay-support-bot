# Panel sessions and devices

The Panel keeps a record of every device currently signed in to your account. Review this list regularly so you always know which devices and locations have access.

## Where to find active sessions

1. Sign in to the Panel.
2. Open **Settings → Security**.
3. Scroll to the active sessions list.

Each entry shows the device, the approximate location, and the time of last activity. Your current session is marked so you can tell it apart from the others.

## Ending a session remotely

To sign out a device you no longer use, or one you do not recognise:

1. Find the session in the list.
2. Select **End session** next to it.

The device is signed out immediately and must authenticate again to return.

## Automatic expiry

Sessions expire automatically after 12 hours of inactivity. Once a session expires, that device has to sign in again, so an unattended device does not stay logged in indefinitely.

## If you see a session you do not recognise

Treat any unfamiliar session as a possible compromise:

1. End that session straight away.
2. Change your account password.
3. Confirm your TOTP two-factor authentication is still active.

If you cannot explain a session and are unsure what to do next, raise a ticket in the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET). Acting quickly limits what an unauthorised device can do while it has access.
