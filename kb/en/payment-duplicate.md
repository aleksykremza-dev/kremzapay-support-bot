# Customer paid twice

Sometimes a buyer completes payment for the same order twice — for example, after refreshing the page or retrying when they were unsure the first attempt worked. If you see two payments in the **completed** status for a single order, only one of them should be kept.

## Confirm both payments

1. Open the **Payments** section.
2. Find both payments linked to the order.
3. Check that both have the **completed** status and cover the same order and amount.

If one payment is still **pending** or has already **expired** or **failed**, no duplicate charge occurred — only completed payments take money.

## Refund the duplicate

When two payments are genuinely completed for one order, refund one of them:

1. Open the **Refunds** section.
2. Create a **full refund** for the duplicate payment.
3. Keep the other payment as the valid one for the order.

Refunds can be issued within the 180-day window, so a recent duplicate is always eligible.

## What to tell the buyer

Let the buyer know that one of the two charges is being refunded in full and that the funds will return to their card within 1–5 business days, depending on their bank. They can track nothing on their side beyond their bank statement; the refund status is visible to you in the **Refunds** section.

If a refund is rejected, contact support through the Panel ticket system or pomoc@kremzapay.demo.
