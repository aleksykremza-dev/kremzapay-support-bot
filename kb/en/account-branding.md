# Branding your payment page

You can personalise the payment page your customers see so it matches your shop. Branding is handled entirely in the Panel — no code changes are needed.

## What you can change

In **Settings → Branding** you can set three things:

- **Logo** — the image shown at the top of the payment page.
- **Brand colour** — the accent colour used for buttons and highlights.
- **Shop display name** — the name your customers see during checkout.

## How to update your branding

1. Sign in to your Panel.
2. Open **Settings → Branding**.
3. Upload your logo, choose your brand colour, and enter your shop display name.
4. Save your changes.

Updates apply within a few minutes. Refresh a payment page or open a new one to confirm the new look.

## Tips for a good result

- Your logo is displayed on a **light background**, so choose a version that stays clearly readable there. A logo designed only for dark backgrounds may be hard to see.
- Use your shop display name that customers will recognise, so the payment step feels like a natural continuation of your checkout.
- Pick a brand colour with enough contrast for buttons to remain easy to read.

Consistent branding reassures customers that they are still paying you, which helps reduce drop-off at the payment step. If your changes do not appear after a few minutes, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
