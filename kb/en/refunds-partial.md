# Partial refunds

A partial refund returns only part of a payment instead of the full amount. This is useful when a customer keeps some items and returns others, or when you agree to a price adjustment after the sale.

To issue a partial refund:

1. Sign in to the kremzaPay Panel.
2. Open the **Payments** section and select the completed payment.
3. Click **Refund**.
4. Enter an amount smaller than the payment total.
5. Confirm the refund.

You can repeat partial refunds on the same payment as many times as needed. Each new refund is limited to the remaining, not-yet-refunded balance. Once the sum of all partial refunds equals the original payment amount, the payment is fully refunded and no further refunds are possible.

Each partial refund is recorded as its own entry in the **Refunds** section and in your reports, with its own amount, date, and status. This lets you reconcile several returns against a single original payment.

Points to remember:

- Partial refunds go back to the original payment method, in the original currency, just like full refunds.
- They are available for 180 days from payment completion, the same window as full refunds.
- Funds reach the customer within 1–5 business days per refund.
- Any fees follow the terms in your agreement.

If you need help splitting a refund or reconciling entries, open a ticket in the Panel or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
