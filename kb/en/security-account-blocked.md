# Why an account gets blocked

A blocked account cannot receive payouts until the block is lifted. Understanding why blocks happen helps you resolve them quickly and avoid them in the first place.

## Common reasons

- **Expired KYC documents** — identity or business verification documents on file have passed their validity date and need to be renewed.
- **Sustained high chargeback rate** — a chargeback rate that stays elevated over time signals risk and can trigger a block.
- **Terms violations** — activity that breaches the service terms.
- **Court or authority order** — a block required by a court or a competent authority.

## How you are notified

When your account is blocked you are notified by email, and a banner appears in the Panel explaining the block. Check both so you know which reason applies and what is required to resolve it.

## What happens to money during a block

- **Payouts are frozen** — funds are not paid out while the block is in place.
- **Buyer refunds are still processed** — customers who are due a refund still receive it, so buyers are not left waiting because of the block.

## How to get unblocked

1. Read the email and Panel banner to confirm the reason.
2. Gather the required documents (for example, renewed KYC documents).
3. Raise a ticket in the Panel, or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET), and attach what is requested.

Support will review your submission and confirm the next steps. Providing complete, current documents the first time is the fastest way to restore payouts.
