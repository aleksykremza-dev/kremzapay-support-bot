# Minimum payout amount

kremzaPay only sends a payout when your available balance reaches at least **10 PLN**. This threshold keeps transfer costs proportionate and avoids sending very small amounts to your bank.

If your balance is below 10 PLN on a scheduled payout day, the funds are not lost. They simply roll over and are added to your next payout, and this continues until the balance clears the 10 PLN minimum.

To see funds that are waiting to be paid out:

1. Open the **Payouts** section in your panel.
2. Look for the **pending balance** figure.
3. This value shows the amount carried over that has not yet reached the threshold.

A few points worth knowing:

- Payouts run on a **T+1 business day** cycle by default once the threshold is met.
- Rollovers are **free** — you are never charged for holding a balance below the minimum until it is paid out.
- Refunds and any deductions are applied before the balance is measured against the threshold, so the pending balance always reflects the true amount owed to you.

If your account regularly settles small sums, expect payouts to be less frequent because several days may combine before crossing 10 PLN. This is normal and requires no action on your part.

If a pending balance seems stuck for an unusually long time, or the figure does not match your completed payments, raise a ticket in the panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET) and we will review it with you.
