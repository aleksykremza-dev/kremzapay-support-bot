# BLIK payments

BLIK is a popular Polish payment method that lets customers pay directly from their banking app using a short one-time code. It is one of the payment methods available through kremzaPay, alongside cards with 3D Secure 2, fast bank transfers, Google Pay, and Apple Pay.

## How a BLIK payment works

1. At checkout, the customer chooses BLIK as the payment method.
2. The customer opens their mobile banking app and generates a **6-digit BLIK code**.
3. The customer enters the code on the payment page.
4. The bank sends a confirmation request to the app. The customer confirms the payment there.
5. Once confirmed, the payment moves to completed and appears in your **Payments** section.

## Important timing

A BLIK code is valid for **2 minutes** from the moment it is generated. If the customer takes too long to enter it, or does not confirm the request in the banking app in time, the code stops working.

## Typical failure: expired code

The most common reason a BLIK payment fails is an **expired code**. If this happens:

- The customer was not charged.
- Ask the customer to generate a fresh 6-digit code and pay again.
- If the payment session itself has expired, the customer will need to start a new payment.
