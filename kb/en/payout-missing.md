# Payout did not arrive

If a payout has not reached your bank account, work through the checklist below before contacting support. Most delays have a simple explanation.

## Checklist

1. **Check the payout status.** In the kremzaPay Panel, go to Payouts and find the payout in question. The status tells you whether it is still pending, has been sent, or was held.
2. **Check the minimum amount.** The minimum payout is 10 PLN. If your balance was below 10 PLN, the amount rolls over to the next payout instead of being sent.
3. **Check the bank account.** Make sure the payout bank account in Settings → Payouts is correct and verified. A pending account change pauses payouts.
4. **Allow for timing.** Payouts follow a T+1 business day schedule and are only sent on business days. Weekends and public holidays shift the date to the next business day.

## When to contact support

If the payout status is "sent" and 2 business days have passed without the funds arriving, contact support. Open a ticket from the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri 8:00–18:00 CET), and include the payout id so we can trace the transfer quickly.
