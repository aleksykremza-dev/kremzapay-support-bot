# What is a chargeback

A chargeback happens when a customer disputes a card payment directly with their bank instead of contacting you. The bank reverses the transaction and pulls the funds back while the dispute is examined. Chargebacks can be raised for reasons such as unrecognized transactions, goods or services not received, or suspected fraud.

## Chargeback vs refund

A chargeback and a refund both return money to the customer, but they work very differently.

- A **refund** is initiated by you, the merchant, from the Refunds section of the Panel. You choose to return the payment, in full or in part, within the 180-day refund window. Refunds usually complete within 1–5 business days.
- A **chargeback** is forced by the customer's bank. You do not start it, and you cannot cancel it directly. Instead, you are notified and given the chance to respond with evidence.

If a customer simply wants their money back and contacts you first, issuing a refund is usually faster and simpler for everyone than a chargeback.

## How you find out

When a chargeback is raised, kremzaPay notifies you in the Panel. The disputed payment is flagged so you can review the details — the amount, the original transaction, and the reason given by the bank.

## What you can do

You can respond to the dispute by submitting evidence that the payment was valid, such as an order confirmation, proof of delivery, or customer correspondence. The bank then reviews your response and makes the final decision. The step-by-step timeline is covered in a separate article on the dispute process.
