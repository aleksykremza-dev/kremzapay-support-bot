# Accepting multiple currencies

kremzaPay lets you accept payments in several currencies. Each shop has its own currency, which determines how prices are charged and how you receive your money.

## Supported currencies

- PLN
- EUR
- USD
- GBP
- CZK

## How the shop currency works

The currency is set **per shop** in your shop configuration. When a buyer pays, the amount is charged in that shop's currency. If you sell in more than one currency, you can set up a separate shop for each currency.

## Payouts

Payouts are always made in the **shop currency**. For example, a shop set to EUR collects payments in EUR and receives payouts in EUR. This keeps your accounting consistent, because the amount you charge and the amount you receive are in the same currency.

Standard payout rules still apply: payouts follow a T+1 schedule with a minimum of 10 PLN, or the equivalent handling for the shop currency as described in your payout settings.

## Buyers paying in another currency

A buyer whose card is issued in a different currency can still pay. In that case, the **buyer's bank** handles the conversion at its own rate, and the buyer sees the converted amount on their statement. This happens entirely on the bank's side, so the amount you receive stays in the shop currency.

If you need a currency that is not on the supported list, open a Panel ticket or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
