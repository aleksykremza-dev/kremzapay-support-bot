# What evidence to collect for a dispute

When a customer disputes a payment, the outcome depends almost entirely on the evidence you submit. Prepare a complete package before you respond, because you cannot add documents once the dispute closes.

Collect the following:

1. **Order confirmation** — the receipt or order record showing the products, price, and transaction date.
2. **Delivery tracking with signature** — proof the goods reached the buyer, ideally with a carrier signature or a delivery confirmation.
3. **Correspondence with the buyer** — emails, chat logs, or messages that show the customer received support and confirmed the order.
4. **Terms acceptance log** — a record that the buyer accepted your terms of sale, refund policy, and delivery conditions at checkout.
5. **Matching buyer and delivery data** — evidence that the name, address, and contact details used for payment match those used for delivery.

The stronger the link between the cardholder and the completed transaction, the better your chances. Screenshots should be legible and dated. Tracking numbers should resolve to a real carrier record.

Submit your evidence within **14 days** through the dispute view inside **Payments** in the Panel. Open the disputed payment, attach each document, and confirm submission. kremzaPay forwards the package to the issuing bank, which usually decides within 30 to 60 days.

If you miss the 14-day window, the dispute is decided without your input and the amount, together with a dispute fee, is deducted from your next payout. Assembling evidence early — even before a dispute arrives — makes every response faster and stronger.
