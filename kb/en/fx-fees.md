# Currency conversion charges

A common question is whether kremzaPay converts currencies and charges for it. The short answer: kremzaPay does not convert between your shop currencies. You are paid out in the currency of the shop that took the payment, without any conversion on our side.

kremzaPay supports payouts in PLN, EUR, USD, GBP and CZK. If your shop operates in one of these currencies, the payment is settled and paid out in that same currency — there is no conversion step, and therefore no conversion charge from kremzaPay.

Conversions can still happen, but they occur outside kremzaPay, in two places:

1. **On the buyer's bank side.** If a customer pays in a currency different from your shop's currency, their own bank or card issuer performs the conversion, using their rate. This is between the buyer and their bank; kremzaPay is not involved and does not set the rate.
2. **On your bank side.** If the currency of your shop differs from the currency of your bank account, your bank converts the incoming payout when it lands, using your bank's rate and terms.

In both cases the conversion — and any charge for it — belongs to the bank performing it, not to kremzaPay.

If you want to avoid third-party conversion entirely, the usual approach is to hold a bank account in the same currency as the shop, so the payout arrives without your bank needing to convert.

For questions about a specific payout amount, check the payout in **Reports** first. If something is unclear, contact us through a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET.
