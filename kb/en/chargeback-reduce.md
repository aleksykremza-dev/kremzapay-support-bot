# Reducing your chargeback rate

Most chargebacks are preventable. A high rate raises costs and can put your account under review, so it is worth building habits that stop disputes before they start.

Focus on the areas that trigger the most complaints:

1. **Use a clear shop name on card statements.** Many disputes come from customers who do not recognise the merchant descriptor. Make sure the name on the statement matches your shop.
2. **Write honest product descriptions.** Match photos, specifications, and delivery times to what you actually ship. Overstated claims lead to "item not as described" disputes.
3. **Show your refund policy.** Keep it visible at checkout and in your terms so customers know they can get money back without involving their bank.
4. **Reply to support quickly.** A customer who reaches you directly rarely opens a chargeback. Slow or missing replies push them toward the bank.
5. **Use tracked delivery.** Tracking with a signature gives you proof of delivery and reassures customers that their order is on the way.
6. **Respond to refund requests before they become disputes.** When someone asks for their money back, act on it. A refund you issue yourself avoids the dispute fee and the rate impact of a chargeback.

Review your disputed payments in the **Payments** section of the Panel to spot patterns — a single product, delivery region, or descriptor may be driving most of them. Fixing the root cause is far more effective than winning individual disputes. A low chargeback rate protects both your payouts and your standing with the card schemes.
