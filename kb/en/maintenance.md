# Planned maintenance

From time to time we perform planned maintenance to update and improve the kremzaPay platform. These windows are scheduled and announced in advance, so they should never come as a surprise.

**How we announce it**

Every planned maintenance window is published in at least two places:

- On the **status page** at status.kremzapay.demo, under planned maintenance
- As a **banner in the Panel**, visible when you log in

Both announcements appear **at least 48 hours before** the work begins, with the exact date and time.

**When it happens**

Maintenance is scheduled during night hours (CET) to keep the impact as low as possible, when transaction volumes are lowest.

**What happens to payments**

During short maintenance windows, payments are not lost. Incoming transactions are queued and processed automatically once the window ends. Statuses then move forward on their own — a payment that was pending during maintenance continues toward authorized or completed afterwards.

**What you need to do**

Nothing. There is no action required from merchants during planned maintenance:

- You do not need to pause your integration
- You do not need to resend transactions
- You do not need to open a ticket

Simply wait for the window to close. If you want a reminder, subscribe to email updates on the status page and you will be notified before each scheduled window. If something still looks wrong well after the announced end time, check the status page again and then open a ticket from the Panel.
