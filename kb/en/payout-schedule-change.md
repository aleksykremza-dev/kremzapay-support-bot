# Changing your payout schedule

You control how often kremzaPay pays out your balance. The schedule is set per shop and can be changed at any time from the Panel.

## Available schedules

- **Daily (T+1)** — the default. Your balance is paid out on the next business day after payments are captured, provided it reaches the 10 PLN minimum.
- **Weekly** — pick a weekday, and your accumulated balance is paid out once a week on that day.
- **Monthly** — your balance is paid out on the first business day of each month.

## How to change it

1. Open **Settings** in the Panel.
2. Go to **Payouts**.
3. Select the schedule you want: daily, weekly, or monthly.
4. For weekly, choose the weekday. For monthly, no extra choice is needed — it uses the first business day.
5. Save your changes.

## When the change takes effect

A new schedule applies **from the next cycle**, not retroactively. The current cycle finishes under the schedule that was active when it started.

Your balance keeps accumulating in the meantime, so nothing is lost while you switch. Whatever has built up is simply paid out on the first payout under the new schedule, as long as it meets the 10 PLN minimum.

You can review upcoming and past payouts at any time in the **Payouts** section. If you have questions about which schedule fits your cash flow, contact us via a Panel ticket or at pomoc@kremzapay.demo, Monday to Friday 8:00–18:00 CET.
