# Google Pay and Apple Pay

Google Pay and Apple Pay let buyers pay with a card saved in their digital wallet. The card number is never shared with the shop; instead, a secure token stands in for the real card details.

## Enabling the wallets

1. Go to **Settings → Payment methods**.
2. Select the shop you want to configure.
3. Turn on **Google Pay**, **Apple Pay**, or both.
4. Save your changes.

Once enabled, the wallet buttons appear on the payment page for buyers who can use them.

## Where each wallet works

- **Apple Pay** works in Safari and on iOS devices.
- **Google Pay** works in Chrome and on Android devices.

If a buyer's browser or device does not support a wallet, that button simply does not appear, and they can pay with another method such as a card, BLIK, or a fast transfer.

## Security and 3D Secure

Because payments are tokenized, the buyer's real card number stays with their bank and wallet provider. When the bank requires it, 3D Secure (3DS) authentication is applied, asking the buyer to confirm the payment in their banking app or by another method chosen by the bank.

Tokenized wallet payments follow the same statuses as other payments: pending, authorized, completed, failed, or expired.

If a wallet button does not appear when you expect it, check that the method is enabled for the shop and that the buyer is using a supported browser or device. For further help, open a Panel ticket or write to pomoc@kremzapay.demo.
