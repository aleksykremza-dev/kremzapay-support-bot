# GDPR / RODO and payment data

kremzaPay handles buyer data in line with data protection rules. This article explains, in plain terms, what data is processed, what you receive as a merchant, and how to make a data request.

## What kremzaPay processes

kremzaPay processes buyer data to the extent required to execute payments. This means the information needed to authenticate the payer, move the funds, and complete and reconcile the transaction — nothing gathered for unrelated purposes.

## What merchants receive

As a merchant you receive only order-related data: the details needed to identify the payment and fulfil the order. You do not receive card numbers or the buyer's banking credentials. Sensitive payment credentials stay within the payment process and never reach your systems.

## How long data is kept

Data is retained for the period required by applicable legal requirements. Payment records must be kept for a defined time, so retention follows those obligations rather than being deleted on request when the law requires it to be kept.

## Making a data request

If you need to make a data request, raise a ticket in the Panel or email pomoc@kremzapay.demo (Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00 CET). Describe what you need so the request can be handled correctly.

## Who controls the data

You remain the controller of your own customer data — the customer relationships and records that belong to your business. For the payment data it processes to execute transactions, kremzaPay acts within its own role under the applicable rules. If you are unsure which data falls where, contact support before acting.
