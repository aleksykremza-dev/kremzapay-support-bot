# Support response times (SLA)

This article explains how quickly you can expect a response from kremzaPay support and how to get your request handled as fast as possible.

**First reply**

Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00–18:00 CET, through a Panel ticket or by email at pomoc@kremzapay.demo. We send a **first reply within 1 business day**. There is no phone support — all requests are handled in writing so that every detail is recorded and traceable.

**Incidents are worked continuously**

Incidents shown on the status page (status.kremzapay.demo) are handled separately from individual tickets. Once an incident is open, our team works on it continuously until it is resolved, and progress is posted to the status page. If your issue matches an active incident there, you do not need a ticket — follow the updates on the status page instead.

**Tickets are prioritized over email**

Requests submitted as a ticket from the Panel are prioritized over messages sent to the email address. Whenever you can, open a ticket from the Panel rather than emailing, so your request is queued with the right context.

**Help us skip the data round-trip**

The single biggest cause of delay is having to ask you for identifying details. To avoid that back-and-forth, include in your first message:

- your **merchant_id**
- the **sessionId** of the affected payment

With these two values we can locate the transaction immediately and start working on the actual problem instead of asking for more information.
